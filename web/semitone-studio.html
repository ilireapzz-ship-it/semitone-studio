The chord brain: a Markov chain

Each row is the chord you are on. Each cell is the chance of moving to the chord in that column. Every row adds up to 100%, which makes it a probability distribution.

P(next = j | now = i) = wij / Σk wik

Outlined cells are the moves used in the current loop. Click any cell to change its weight (0 → 4). Raise a cell and that move becomes more likely next time you press New idea.

The melody uses weights too: a note scores higher if it is a small step from the last note, and three times higher if it lands on a chord tone on a strong beat (beats 1 and 3).

    Frequency

    Each semitone multiplies frequency by 21/12 ≈ 1.05946. Twelve of them double it: one octave.

    Interval

    First 25 ms of both waves (thin lines) and their sum (thick line). Simple ratios like 3:2 make a sum that repeats quickly. That regular pattern is what we hear as consonance.

    Chords live inside the scale

    Stack every other note (degrees 1-3-5). The gaps decide the type: 4+3 semitones is major, 3+4 is minor, 3+3 is diminished. Click to hear.

    Why twelve notes?

    Stack perfect fifths (×3/2) and you never land exactly on an octave (×2). Twelve fifths come very close to seven octaves. Western tuning closes that small gap by making every fifth a tiny bit flat, so all twelve keys sound equally in tune.

    Tempo turns beats into milliseconds

    Note lengths are fractions of a whole note: each one halves the one above, so they are powers of 2. A dot adds half again (×1.5). A triplet squeezes three into the space of two (×2/3). Producers use these exact times for delay and reverb so effects lock to the beat.

    Polyrhythm: two grids, one cycle

    against

    Amber follows the beat. Blue fits its hits evenly into the same span. The least common multiple (LCM) is the smallest grid where both land on whole cells.

    Swing is a ratio

    Straight 50%Triplet 67%Hard 75%

    Each beat splits into a long and a short eighth note. At 2:1 (67%) you get the classic triplet shuffle. Most hip-hop and R&B grooves sit between 55% and 62%.

    Steps on the staff

    Each line or space is the next letter. Line to the next space is one step up the white keys. Middle C sits on one ledger line between the two staves.

    Mixer & effects

    One strip per instrument. Set its level and its place in the stereo field (orchestras are seated: violins left, cellos and basses right), and add up to three effects. To shape a part over time, press A on its row above to open an automation lane.

    Effects and moves chosen for the template’s character, plus crescendos that follow the dynamics plan.
    Orchestral music usually wants Off or Gentle: wide dynamics are the point.
    Orchestra sounds built-in synths, modelled instruments or your own SoundFonts

    Piano and harp use the modelled instruments by default, and pizzicato strings always use the plucked-string model. A free orchestral SoundFont gives the biggest step up; choices are remembered in this browser.

    Full score

    Written at concert pitch, as most film scores are, so every instrument shows the notes that sound. Instrument families are bracketed in standard score order: woodwinds, brass, percussion, harp and keys, choir, strings. Violas read the alto clef.
